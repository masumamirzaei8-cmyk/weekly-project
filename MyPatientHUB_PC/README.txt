MyPatientHUB - PC Version

HOW TO OPEN:
1. Extract this folder anywhere on your PC.
2. Double-click START_MY_PATIENT_HUB.bat.
3. If Windows asks what app to use, choose your browser (Chrome or Edge).

You can also double-click index.html directly.

Files:
- index.html
- public/style.css
- public/login.css
- public/login.js
- public/script.js

The project is designed to work locally without a server and does not require npm or installation.
